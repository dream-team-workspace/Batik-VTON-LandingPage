Android · Virtual batik try-on

Try Indonesian batik on your portrait.

CoBatik presents Batik VTON, an Android app for exploring a curated batik archive, capturing a portrait and previewing a batik look generated by an AI model on the server.

How it works / 01

From a batik archive to a personal preview.

Choose a piece, take a portrait in the app, then see the selected batik visualized on your photo.

  1. 01

    Explore the archive

    Browse batik traditions from Cirebon, Yogyakarta, Central Java, Solo and more.

  2. 02

    Choose a garment

    Select a long-sleeve shirt, formal suit, linen set or jarit.

  3. 03

    Take a portrait

    Capture a photo in the app. It corrects orientation, resizes and compresses the image before upload.

  4. 04

    View the try-on

    A server-side model visualizes the selected batik on your portrait and returns a preview.

The archive / 02

Each piece carries a place and a story.

The curated archive pairs batik garments with their regional origins, stories and meanings.

Mega Mendung · Sawunggaling · Tugu Kembang Kreasi · Burung Hong

Regions

Cirebon, Yogyakarta, Central Java, Solo and other batik traditions.

Garments

Long-sleeve shirts, formal suits, linen sets and jarit.

Virtual fitting

The app uses FASHN VTON 1.5 on a server. The project page notes that the try-on model needs GPU memory, so image generation happens off the phone.

Curated boutique / 03

Selected from Engrasia.

The project page describes this archive as curated from Engrasia, an Indonesian batik tulis boutique. It features hand-drawn cloth from Cirebon, Pekalongan, Lasem and Yogyakarta, selected from the original collection.

Photo handling / 04

A portrait is used for the preview.

These details follow the app project page’s description of its photo handling.

No account or tracking

The app page says no registration, login, analytics SDK or advertising identifier is used.

Temporary uploads

Portraits are uploaded to generate a try-on result; temporary files are deleted after each request.

No saved history

The project page says results are not saved, history is not retained and no profile is created.

Processed, not archived

Images are sent to the try-on model to produce a result, then discarded, according to the app page.
