How it works

Four steps, one portrait.

  1. 01

    Explore the archive

    Traditions from Cirebon, Yogyakarta, Central Java, Solo and beyond — with the story behind each motif.

  2. 02

    Choose a garment

    A long-sleeve shirt, a formal suit, a linen set, or a jarit.

  3. 03

    Take a portrait

    Shoot in-app. The photo is straightened, resized and compressed before it ever uploads.

  4. 04

    See the try-on

    A server-side model fits the motif onto your portrait and returns the preview.

The cloth

Hand-drawn, then worn.

The archive is curated from Engrasia, an Indonesian batik tulis boutique. Its hand-drawn cloth comes from Cirebon, Pekalongan, Lasem and Yogyakarta — wax, dye and patient hands, one panel at a time.

Every motif is older than the phone in your hand. CoBatik does not redraw the cloth — it puts the real thing on you.

Photo handling

Your portrait, processed — not kept.

No account, no tracking
No registration, no login, no analytics SDK and no advertising identifier.
Temporary uploads
The portrait is uploaded only to generate the try-on; temporary files are deleted after each request.
Nothing saved
Results are not stored, no history is kept, and no profile is built.
Processed, then discarded
Images reach the model to produce a preview, and leave with it.
